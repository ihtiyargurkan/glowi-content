Why does your skin feel oilier the more you wash it?

The popular answer: your skin panics and makes more oil. But the evidence for that is thin.

Oil comes from glands deep in your skin, and hormones set their pace. Washing the surface doesn't reach them.

What washing does change is the top layer. Strip it too often, and the barrier cracks. Skin turns tight, red and flaky, while oil keeps flowing from below.

And oil comes back within a few hours anyway. Every extra wash just restarts the clock.

In one small acne trial, washing twice a day beat once a day, and four times added nothing.

So wash twice a day with a gentle cleanser and lukewarm water. Blot shine instead of washing again, and still moisturize.
