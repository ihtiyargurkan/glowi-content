Why does sugar age your skin?

Not overnight. It works slowly, through a process called glycation.

Collagen is the spring that keeps your skin firm. When there's extra sugar in your blood, it sticks to those fibers.

Over time, it glues neighboring fibers together. Scientists call these bonds AGEs, advanced glycation end products.

Glued fibers can't stretch, and they're hard to repair. Springy collagen turns stiff, like caramel that's cooled. Sunlight speeds it up.

In a study of 600 people, every extra unit of blood sugar made them look about 5 months older to strangers.

So keep your blood sugar steady. Cut back on sweet drinks, pair carbs with fiber and protein, and wear sunscreen every day.
