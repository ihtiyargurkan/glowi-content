Why does your skin age, even on days you never see the sun?

It's not just time. It's light you can't feel.

Sunlight carries two kinds of UV. UVB burns the surface. You feel it.

UVA is quieter. Up to 80% of UV slips through light clouds, and UVA passes right through window glass.

No burn. No warning. It dives deep, into the layer where collagen lives.

Collagen is the spring under your skin. UVA snaps it, Fiber by fiber.

Your body repairs some of it. Not all. The gaps add up: fine lines, dark spots, dull texture.

In one study, people who used sunscreen daily for 4.5 years showed 24% less skin aging.

So the fix is boring. Broad-spectrum SPF_30+ every morning, cloudy or not.
