Why is your skin still dry, even when you drink 8 glasses of water a day?

Because water doesn't go straight to your face.

It reaches your blood first, then every organ. Your skin gets its share from below.

But the outer layer isn't filled from a glass. It's a wall of dead cells held together by fats, like bricks and mortar.

When the mortar is healthy, water stays in. When it cracks, water slips out into the air, and dry air pulls it out faster.

Drinking more still helps a little. In one study, women who added 2 liters a day for a month had better skin hydration, especially those who drank little before.

But a review of 6 studies called the evidence weak.

So drink enough, then seal it in. Moisturize right after washing, look for glycerin or hyaluronic acid, and keep showers short.
