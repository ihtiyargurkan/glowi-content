Does your morning coffee dry out your skin?

Coffee is famous for dehydrating you. For regular drinkers, that's mostly a myth.

Only big doses of caffeine make your kidneys flush out extra water. Daily drinkers build a tolerance, and a mug of coffee is mostly water anyway.

In a study of 50 men who drink coffee daily, 4 mugs a day kept them just as hydrated as the same amount of water.

The real catch is timing. In another study, a big dose of caffeine taken 6 hours before bed cut sleep by more than an hour.

And a sleepless night shows on your face: paler skin, darker circles and puffier eyes.

So keep your coffee. Have your last cup early, at least 6 hours before bed, add a glass of water, and moisturize after you wash.
