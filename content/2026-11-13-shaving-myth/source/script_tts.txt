Does shaving make your hair grow back thicker?

It's one of the oldest beauty myths. Scientists tested it in nineteen twenty-eight, and again in nineteen seventy.

In the nineteen seventy study, five men shaved one leg every week and left the other leg alone. Hair weight, width and growth speed didn't change.

So why does stubble feel thicker? A natural hair narrows to a fine, soft tip. A razor slices through the thicker middle and leaves a blunt end.

That blunt end feels coarse. And short regrowth stands out darker against your skin.

The hair itself grows from a root deep under your skin. A razor never reaches it.

So shave for comfort. Wet your skin first, use a gel, shave with the grain, and swap blades every five to seven shaves.
