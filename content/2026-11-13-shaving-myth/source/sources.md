# Sources — shaving and hair thickness (opened 2026-11-05)
1. Lynfield YL, Macwilliams P. Shaving and hair growth. J Invest Dermatol. 1970. https://paperguide.ai/papers/527af220-24f2-4e77-a629-2ff67387f1e8-shaving-and-hair-growth (PubMed 5459955)
   - 5 healthy young men; one leg shaved weekly, other leg untreated control; measured total hair weight per area, hair width and growth rate → no significant differences attributable to shaving.
2. Trotter M. Hair growth and shaving. Anat Rec. 1928 — as summarised by LiveScience: https://livescience.com/22462-does-shaving-really-cause-hair-to-grow-back-thicker-and-coarser.html
   - shaving had no effect on hair colour, texture or growth rate. Same article explains why stubble seems thicker: natural hair tapers to a fine tip, shaving cuts the thicker shaft leaving a blunt end; short cut hair contrasts darker against skin.
3. American Academy of Dermatology, "Hair removal: How to shave". https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/skin-care-basics/hair/how-to-shave
   - wet skin and hair first, use shaving cream or gel, shave in the direction hair grows, rinse after each swipe, change blade / discard razor after 5–7 shaves → script line.
