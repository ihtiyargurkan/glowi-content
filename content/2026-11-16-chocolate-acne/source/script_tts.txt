Does chocolate really make you break out?

For decades, the answer was no. A nineteen sixty-nine study found no link. But its fake chocolate bar was loaded with fat and sugar too.

So newer studies used real cocoa. In twenty sixteen, twenty-five acne-prone young men ate twenty-five grams of ninety-nine percent dark chocolate every day for four weeks.

Within two weeks, they had more blackheads and more red bumps.

Why? A lab study offers a clue. After people ate chocolate, their immune cells reacted more strongly to acne bacteria, like a smoke alarm set too sensitive.

But these studies were small, mostly men, and the twenty sixteen one had no comparison group. So chocolate is a possible trigger, not a proven cause.

So test it yourself. Skip it for a few weeks, then bring it back, and watch your skin.
