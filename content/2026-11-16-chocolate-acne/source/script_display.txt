Does chocolate really make you break out?

For decades, the answer was no. A 1969 study found no link. But its fake chocolate bar was loaded with fat and sugar too.

So newer studies used real cocoa. In 2016, 25 acne-prone young men ate 25 grams of 99% dark chocolate every day for 4 weeks.

Within 2 weeks, they had more blackheads and more red bumps.

Why? A lab study offers a clue. After people ate chocolate, their immune cells reacted more strongly to acne bacteria, like a smoke alarm set too sensitive.

But these studies were small, mostly men, and the 2016 one had no comparison group. So chocolate is a possible trigger, not a proven cause.

So test it yourself. Skip it for a few weeks, then bring it back, and watch your skin.
