# Sources — stress breakouts (opened 2026-11-04)
1. Yosipovitch G, et al. Study of psychological stress, sebum production and acne vulgaris in adolescents. Acta Derm Venereol. 2007;87(2):135-9. https://medicaljournalssweden.se/actadv/article/view/8339
   - 94 secondary-school students (mean age 14.9), exam period vs summer holidays. "Sebum measurements did not differ significantly between the high stress and low stress conditions." Stress correlated with papulopustular acne severity (r=0.23, p=0.029).
2. Chiu A, Chon SY, Kimball AB. The response of skin disease to stress: changes in the severity of acne vulgaris as affected by examination stress. Arch Dermatol. 2003;139(7):897-900. https://research.bidmc.org/alexa-kimball/publications/response-skin-disease-stress-changes-severity-acne-vulgaris-affected-examination
   - 22 university students; acne severity rose with exam stress (r=0.61) after adjusting for sleep and diet changes.
3. Garg A, et al. Psychological stress perturbs epidermal permeability barrier homeostasis. Arch Dermatol. 2001;137(1):53-9. https://pesquisa.bvsalud.org/enfermeria/resource/pt/mdl-11176661
   - 27 students; barrier recovery after tape stripping was slower during final exams than before/after.
On-screen: oil/acne tube heights and repair bars are labelled "illustrative scale"; the only number is 94 (students in Yosipovitch 2007) and 27 (Garg 2001 in the footer).
4. Zouboulis CC / Böhm M et al. Neuroendocrine regulation of sebocytes — a pathogenetic link between stress and acne. Exp Dermatol. 2004;13 Suppl 4:31-5. PMID 15507110 (abstract read via https://api-pubmed-ncbi.laibokeji.com/15507110)
   - Sebocytes carry receptors for stress mediators (CRH, melanocortins, beta-endorphin...), which can drive inflammatory cytokines, lipid production, proliferation. Supports "Stress hormones CAN put your skin on alert" (wording softened).
5. "Debunking acne myths: does popping pimples resolve acne faster?" Cutis (MDedge), 2017. https://mdedge.com/content/debunking-acne-myths-does-popping-pimples-resolve-acne-faster
   - Picking/squeezing leaves lesions "inflamed and far more visible" and slower to heal; raises scarring risk. Supports "Picking leaves spots redder, and slower to heal."
