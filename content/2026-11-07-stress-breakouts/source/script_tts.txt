Why does stress make you break out?

The popular answer: stress floods your face with oil. But when researchers followed ninety-four students into exam season, their oil levels stayed about the same.

Their acne still got worse. So the trigger isn't just oil.

Stress hormones can put your skin on alert. Inside a clogged pore, a small spark turns into a red, swollen bump.

Stress also slows repair. In another study, students' skin barrier healed more slowly during final exams.

And stressed hands find your face. Picking leaves spots redder, and slower to heal.

So calm your skin, not just your mind. Keep your routine gentle, keep your hands off spots, protect your sleep, and give breakouts time.
