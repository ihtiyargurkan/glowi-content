Why does city air show up on your skin?

Not as dirt you can see. Traffic and smoke fill the air with particles much finer than a hair.

They settle on your face all day and can slip into your pores. There, they spark a slow chemical stress, a bit like rust.

That stress can wake up your pigment cells. They make extra color, and it gathers into dark spots.

In a German study of 400 women, every step up in soot came with 20% more dark spots on the cheeks.

It's a link, not proof, and the women were 70 to 80. But dark spots showed the clearest signal.

So wash the day off every night. And wear sunscreen every morning, because sunlight drives dark spots too.
