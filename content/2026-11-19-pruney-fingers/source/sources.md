# Sources — Why do your fingers wrinkle in the bath?
1. Kareklas K, Nettle D, Smulders TV. Water-induced finger wrinkles improve handling of wet objects. Biol Lett. 2013. Newcastle University press release https://www.ncl.ac.uk/press/articles/archive/2013/01/getagrip-whywegetwrinklyfingersandtoes.html : hands soaked in warm water for 30 minutes; wet marbles moved 12% faster with wrinkled fingers; no difference for dry objects; wrinkles caused by blood vessels constricting below the skin, controlled by the autonomic nervous system. Live Science https://www.livescience.com/26097-why-fingers-pruney-water.html : 20 volunteers; scientists once thought the puckering came from the outer layer absorbing water and swelling.
2. The Conversation (Kareklas/Smulders) https://theconversation.com/how-finger-wrinkles-help-us-handle-slippery-stuff-11495 : "osmosis is only the first step, not the entire explanation"; process is neurally controlled, constricting blood vessels.
3. Nature News 2013 https://www.nature.com/articles/nature.2013.12175 : "researchers have known since the 1930s that the effect does not occur when there is nerve damage in the fingers"; Changizi et al. (2011) proposed the wrinkles form a drainage network that improves grip.
