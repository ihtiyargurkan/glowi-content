MYTH

"Toothpaste dries out pimples"

It's made for teeth. On skin it can leave a redder, more irritated spot than you started with.

INSTEADA spot product made for skin, like salicylic acid.
GLOWI3/8
MYTH

"Natural means gentle"

Plant oils and fragrances can still cause reactions. Poison ivy is natural too.

INSTEADJudge the ingredient list, not the leaf on the label.
GLOWI4/8
MYTH

"Scrub harder, get cleaner"

Washcloths, sponges and hard scrubbing irritate skin. Clean isn't the same as squeaky.

INSTEADFingertips and a gentle cleanser.
GLOWI5/8
MYTH

"It should work in a week"

Most products need several weeks to a few months before you see a difference.

INSTEADGive one product time before you swap it out.
GLOWI6/8
HALF TRUE

"If it tingles, it's working"

Retinol and glycolic acid can sting at first, and that's often temporary. Redness, itching or swelling isn't progress.

VERDICTMild and fading: fine. Red, itchy, swollen: stop.
