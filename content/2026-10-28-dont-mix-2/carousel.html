01

Retinol + face waxing

Retinol
+
Wax

Retinoids make skin more fragile, so wax can lift skin along with the hair.

INSTEADStop retinol 2–5 days before waxing your face.
GLOWI3/7
02

Benzoyl peroxide + colored fabric

Benzoyl peroxide
+
Colored fabric

It can bleach colored towels, pillowcases and hair.

INSTEADUse white towels and pillowcases, and let it dry first.
GLOWI4/7
03

Lime juice + sunshine

Lime / lemon
+
Sun

Citrus juice on skin plus UV can cause a rash, blisters and dark marks that last for months.

INSTEADWash it off with soap and water as soon as you can.
GLOWI5/7
04

SPF 30 + SPF 30

SPF 30 moisturizer
≠
SPF 30 foundation

Layering SPF products doesn't add up to 60. You get roughly the highest one you used.

INSTEADOne proper sunscreen layer, SPF 30+, applied generously.
