"Hypoallergenic"

There's no federal standard for it in the US. Each brand decides what it means, and doesn't have to prove it to the FDA.

SOPatch test anyway.
GLOWI3/7
02

"Broad spectrum"

SPF mostly measures UVB. Broad spectrum means it was tested to protect against UVA too.

SOLook for both: SPF 30+ and broad spectrum.
GLOWI4/7
03

"Water resistant"

US labels can only say 40 or 80 minutes. There's no such thing as a waterproof sunscreen.

SOReapply after that time when you swim or sweat.
