Can face exercises make you look younger?

It sounds like a gimmick. But one small study actually put it to the test.

16 women aged 40 to 65 did 30 minutes of face exercises a day for 8 weeks, then every other day until week 20.

Doctors who didn't know which photo came first guessed their ages. The average guess dropped from 50.8 to 48.1.

The researchers think it's muscle. Under your skin, cheek muscles work like cushions under a sheet. Train them, and they get fuller, lifting the fabric above.

But it was only 16 women, with no comparison group. So it's a promising hint, not proof.

If you try it, be consistent, and keep the basics. Daily sunscreen has far stronger proof against skin aging.
