# Sources — face exercises (opened 2026-11-04)
1. Alam M, et al. Association of facial exercise with the appearance of aging. JAMA Dermatol. 2018. Primary abstract opened: https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamadermatology/article-abstract/2666801 (plus summary https://mdedge.com/content/facial-exercises-improved-appearance-small-study-middle-aged-women)
   - 27 enrolled, 16 completed, women aged 40–65; two 90-min training sessions, 30 min/day for 8 weeks, then every other day through week 20.
   - Mean perceived age (blinded raters): 50.8 at week 0, 49.6 at week 8, 48.1 at week 20 → on screen "50.8 → 48.1".
   - Caveats: small sample, no control group, self-selected. Authors suggest cheek-muscle hypertrophy (not directly tested) → "The researchers think it's muscle", labelled illustrative.
2. Hughes MCB, Williams GM, Baker P, Green AC. Sunscreen and prevention of skin aging: a randomized trial. Ann Intern Med. 2013;158(11):781-790. https://read.qxmd.com/read/23732711/sunscreen-and-prevention-of-skin-aging-a-randomized-trial
   - 903 adults, 4.5 years; daily broad-spectrum sunscreen group showed no detectable increase in skin aging; 24% less aging than discretionary use (OR 0.76). Supports "Daily sunscreen has far stronger proof against skin aging" (large RCT vs a 16-person uncontrolled study). No figure shown on screen.
