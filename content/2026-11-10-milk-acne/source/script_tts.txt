Does milk really give you breakouts?

The honest answer is maybe, a little. A review of fourteen studies with seventy-eight thousand five hundred twenty-nine young people found that dairy drinkers had about twenty-five percent higher odds of acne.

And it wasn't only whole milk. Skim milk and yogurt showed the link too.

One idea is hormones. Milk raises insulin and a growth signal called I-G-F-one, which can push oil glands to work harder.

Picture a dimmer switch on each oil gland. These signals may turn it up a notch, not to full blast.

But every study only watched people. None proved that milk causes acne, and some skin may simply be more sensitive.

So don't cut every food at once. If you suspect milk, have less of it for a few weeks, keep your routine the same, and track your skin.
