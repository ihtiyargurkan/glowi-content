# Sources — milk and breakouts (opened 2026-11-04)
1. Juhl CR, Bergholdt HKM, Miller IM, Jemec GBE, Kanters JK, Ellervik C. Dairy intake and acne vulgaris: a systematic review and meta-analysis of 78,529 children, adolescents, and young adults. Nutrients. 2018;10(8):1049. https://mdpi.com/2072-6643/10/8/1049
   - 14 studies, 78,529 participants aged 7–30. Pooled unadjusted ORs: any dairy 1.25 (1.15–1.36) → "about 25% higher odds"; any milk 1.28; whole milk 1.22; low-fat/skim milk 1.32; yogurt 1.36; cheese 1.22 (borderline).
   - All observational (cross-sectional, case-control, retrospective, longitudinal); no RCTs; causality not established; publication bias (trim-and-fill 1.16).
   - Mechanism proposed in the paper: milk amino acids promote insulin secretion and hepatic IGF-1; IGF-1 can induce sebum; mTORC1 signalling. Shown on screen as "illustrative · proposed mechanism".
