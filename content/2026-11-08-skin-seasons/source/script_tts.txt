Why does your skin act differently every season?

It isn't random. Your skin's outer layer is always trading water with the air around it.

In summer, warm, humid air keeps that layer plump. Oil flows more easily, so skin can look shinier.

In winter, cold air holds little water, and indoor heating dries it even more. The air pulls water out of your skin, and the barrier turns brittle.

That makes winter skin more reactive. In a study of four hundred eighty-seven people, the same mild irritant raised water loss about three times more in January than in August.

So let your routine follow the weather. In cold, dry months, use a richer moisturizer and a gentler cleanser. In humid months, go lighter, and keep sunscreen all year.
